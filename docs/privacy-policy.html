Privacy Policy — WaterLab Turbidimeter

Effective 25 October 2026. Publisher: Alex Manuel Rivera Rivera (Cuban Water Lab). Privacy contact: cubanwaterlab@gmail.com.

What the app does

WaterLab Turbidimeter connects to a compatible USB-C water turbidity sensor when you choose “Connect sensor” and grant Android's USB permission. It requests a reading when you choose “Measure now”.

Data on your phone

The app saves sensor readings in its private app storage: UTC time, value, unit, quality status, calibration identifier, raw optical ratio, and sensor USB identity (vendor, product, and serial number when available). It also stores calibration curves, their five sensor readings per sample, reference values, units, capture times, and reference instrument details you enter. It uses these records to show History and to let you share a CSV. It does not create an account or access your location. Contributor details entered in an earlier app version may remain in private app storage until you delete local data or uninstall the app.

Network and sharing

This version has no Internet permission and does not send readings or contributor details to the publisher or a server. It has no advertising or analytics SDKs. If you choose “Share readings (CSV)”, Android opens a sharing chooser and passes the CSV to the app you select. That app's privacy practices apply to the copy you share. You can also export calibration JSON files to a location you select through Android’s file picker, or import a calibration file for the same sensor. Exported files contain the sensor identity and reference details; their storage and sharing are under your control.

Retention, deletion, and security

Readings, calibration records, and any older contributor details remain on your phone until you choose Privacy > Delete all local data or uninstall the app. Deleting local data does not delete previously shared or exported copies. Android backup is disabled. The data is stored in Android's app-private storage; access to the USB sensor requires Android's permission dialog. The publisher does not retain a server copy because this version has no upload service.

Questions

Contact cubanwaterlab@gmail.com with privacy questions. This policy will be updated before any future version adds online data submission or location access.
