Turbimeter is a companion app for a compatible USB-C water turbidity sensor. It communicates with the sensor when you tap Connect sensor and grant Android's USB permission.

## Data stored on your phone

The app stores readings in its private app storage: UTC time, value, unit, quality status, and calibration identifier. If you fill in Signature, it stores your name and optional institution or group in private app preferences. The app does not create an account or store your precise location.

## Network and sharing

This version does not automatically send readings or profile details to the publisher or a server. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. If you choose Share readings (CSV), Android opens a sharing chooser and passes the CSV to the app you select. That recipient's privacy practices then apply to the copy you shared.

## Retention and deletion

Readings and Signature details remain on your phone until you select Privacy > Delete all local data or uninstall the app. Android backup is disabled for this app. We do not retain a server copy because this version has no upload service.

## Security

The data is kept in Android app-private storage. The app has no release-version internet permission. Access to the USB sensor requires the permission shown by Android.
